At Thalia Venturis Partners, our mission is to unlock Suriname's wealth of opportunities for equity investors seeking high-growth sectors in an emerging market. The country is undergoing economic transformation with a focus on diversification beyond traditional extractive industries.

Thalia Venturis Partners is a venture capital and growth consulting firm established to identify, fund, and guide innovative local Surinamese companies positioned for transformative global markets. Our mission is to empower and accelerate growth in both emerging startups and acquisitions, with a commitment to sustainable value creation and ethical governance in the country of Suriname.

We operate at the intersection of capital, strategy, and innovation — supporting ventures that demonstrate strong leadership, scalable business models, and a clear contribution to technological, economic, and social progress.
