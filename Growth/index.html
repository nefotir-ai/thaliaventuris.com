Growth

We seek to partner, invest and transform local startups into market leaders. Providing equity capital investments and digital expertise to various growth sectors such as AI, ArgiTech, EnergyTech, FinTech and eduTech.

Digitalization

Invest in early-stage or growth-stage startups, provide infrastructure or expertise in exchange for equity.

High Potential: Software development, BPO/KPO services, AI Model & Cloud Services.

AI Acceleration

Build a startup ecosystem that guides AI ventures via Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) embedded with enterprise clients, to ship Agentic AI systems through rigorous MLOps harnessing.

High Potential: Oil & Gas, AgriTech, Government and Banking.

Renewable Energy

Invest in Independent Power Producer (IPP) ventures, support local energy entrepreneurs.

High Potential: Solar power, mini-grids, and hybrid energy systems for rural areas.

Eco-Tourism & Hospitality

Form joint ventures with local tour operators or fund sustainable hospitality projects.

High Potential: Rainforest lodges, cultural tourism, river cruises.

Real Estate & Infrastructure

Co-develop or fund construction projects and material suppliers (cement, metakaolin, tiles).

High Potential: Urban housing, mixed-use developments, industrial parks.

Healthcare & Education

Invest in scalable models for regional medical tourism and edtech solutions.

High Potential: Private clinics, diagnostic labs, online education platforms.

Agri-business

Partner with SMEs lacking access to capital to expand production, improve processing facilities, and scale for export.

High Potential: Rice, cassava, fruits, and aquaculture.
